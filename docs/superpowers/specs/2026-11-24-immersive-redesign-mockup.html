Desktop

Phone (390 × 844)

What changed, and why. The old version had three big sizes fighting: the page title (56 px), the row titles (48 px) and the outlined titles, and a tiny line of text floating far to the right. Now there is one type scale (12 · 17 · 23 · 37 · 63 px at 1440 wide): the page title steps down to a quiet 37 px heading, so the four collection names are clearly the largest thing on the page. Each row sits on a 12-column grid: number, name with its season and theme directly under it (and the Sinhala name for Mano Padana), then her line with what's inside ("17 photographs · 8 spreads"), and an arrow button that fills on hover. Hovered rows no longer turn into thin outlines (hard to read); the others simply fade. The phone uses the same scale: 30 px heading, 26 px names opening to 34 px.

About her is now one short paragraph and three facts (studying, experience, recognition), plus a LinkedIn link. Her title is "Fashion designer". Her moodboard sits in fixed frames that change photograph in turn, so nothing has to slide in from off-screen on a phone. Skills are sewing-pattern pieces on her cutting mat: Craft on a bodice front (cut 1 on fold), Design on a skirt panel, Tools on a sleeve, People on a patch pocket. Each has a grain-line arrow and a seam allowance that sews itself in once the pieces have settled; a tape measure lies across the corner. The pieces drop onto the mat one after another and lift when you point at them; the text itself never moves. On the phone they lie one under another down the mat. The contact card now follows the end of the page like the next block, so it can no longer slide over the last section.

Inside a collection, v3

Pick a collection. Wheel over either screen to scroll; click photos, pages and buttons. (Clicking a row on the Collections tab opens it here too.)

Desktop

Phone (390 × 844)

One page per collection, two ways in. The opening is her first photographs at full height, in proportion, drifting slowly, with two buttons: The shoot and The portfolio. Once you're past it, a small switch floats at the bottom of the screen, so you can jump between the two at any time.
The shoot. Every photograph. It opens as the ring from your brief, turning slowly as you scroll, with a pause button. Drag to spin it. Switch to Grid to see them all at once, uncropped. Click any photo for the full-screen viewer, with arrows, the keyboard, swipe on phones, and a caption for each.
The portfolio. A page-by-page reader. Pages stand in a stack, and turning one tips the front page forward so it lands flat, revealing the next. On desktop a magnifier follows the cursor for small writing. Click (or tap on the phone) to zoom to 2.5× and explore by moving the mouse or dragging. Scroll or use + and − to zoom further, and Esc to close. On the phone the double spreads are split into single pages so the text is bigger. Where her page text exists, Read the text opens it as real text.
The end leads to the next collection in the journey.
